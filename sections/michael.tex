\section{Michael}
Clear visual hierarchy and structured data representation enhance the readability of empirical reports.
In APA 7, introduce technical terms or key terminology with \textit{italics} upon first mention rather than boldface.
Reserve \textbf{bold text} primarily for table titles, section headings, and specific mathematical vectors.

\subsection{Text Formatting and Lists}
Unordered lists are appropriate when the sequence of elements has no inherent chronological or ranked order.
In APA 7, list items that form complete sentences must begin with a capital letter and conclude with appropriate punctuation:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Selection pressures govern the reproductive probability of candidate solutions.
    \item Crossover operators facilitate the recombination of genetic material between parents.
    \item Mutation mechanisms maintain population diversity across evolutionary epochs.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{APA 7 Compliant Tables}
Tables in APA 7 omit vertical borders and rely strictly on horizontal rules at the top, header baseline, and footer.
The table number appears in bold, followed by a double-spaced title in italicized title case.
Table notes positioned immediately below the table provide abbreviations and explanatory context.
